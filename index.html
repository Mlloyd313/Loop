Which golfer are you?

Loop prices every shot for one golfer: how far each club goes, how the shots spread, and what it takes to hole out from where they stop. Pick the nearest; Settings → You fine-tunes it or takes your own clubs.

From published data only: TrackMan's PGA TOUR averages, Shot Scope's averages by handicap, Broadie's Golfmetrics (2008) for how shots spread at each level; the drive widths are the model's (ESTIMATED). Model strokes, never a score prediction.
